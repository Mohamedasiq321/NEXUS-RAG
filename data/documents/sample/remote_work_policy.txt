NEXUS Corporation - Remote Work Policy 2026

Policy ID: HR-RW-2026-001
Version: 2026.1
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Department: Human Resources

1. Eligibility

Employees who have completed their probation period may be eligible for remote work, subject to their role, manager approval, and business requirements.

2. Remote Work Allowance

Eligible employees may work remotely for up to three working days per week. Employees must remain available during their assigned working hours.

3. Equipment

Employees approved for remote work may request company-provided equipment according to their role and department requirements. Equipment requests must be approved by the employee's manager.

4. Security Requirements

Employees working remotely must use company-approved devices, maintain secure network connections, and follow all information security policies.

5. Exceptions

Certain roles may require regular physical presence at company facilities. Exceptions to the standard remote work allowance require approval from the department head.

6. Policy Review

This policy will be reviewed annually by the Human Resources department.

Source: NEXUS Corporation Human Resources
